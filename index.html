How it works

  1. Seeds first. Once the play-in tournament ends (mid-April 2027), the real 1-8 seeds replace the TBD slots on this page.
  2. Picks lock before Game 1. Fill every series, copy the text, paste it in the group chat to Miguel before the first round tips off.
  3. Scoring is automatic. As series end, results go into the site and the leaderboard scores itself.
  4. Winner takes the pot. Most points wins. Tiebreak: correct champion, then correct Finals teams, then earliest submission.
